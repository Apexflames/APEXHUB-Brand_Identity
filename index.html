Ascent Node won because it can be explained in one sentence and holds up as a silhouette with no color at all.

The mark: Ascent Node

One sentence: an A rises between the two pillars of an H, and a single node marks the apex.

HUB is the H: two structural pillars with a connection between them. APEX is the peak that rises from that connection, topped by a node that sits level with the pillar tops. The chevron is both the crossbar of the H and the strokes of the A, so neither letter is added; each is revealed by the other.

Technology: pillars and a node read as structure and a point on a network, with no circuit or bracket clichés.

Grid 100 units. Pillar width 16 (x). Chevron stroke 14. Node radius 6.5. Clear space = 1x on all sides, with x = pillar width.

Primary and secondary

Primary: stacked or horizontal mark plus wordmark. Secondary: the compact horizontal lockup for headers and tight layouts.

Monogram and icon

The mark itself is the A+H monogram. It needs no separate construction, which keeps one symbol across every surface.

Micro mark

Node removed, pillars widened to 22 and chevron to 20. Used at 24px and below, and for the favicon.

Wordmark

Sora Bold for APEX, Sora Light for HUB, tracking +70. The weight shift mirrors the mark: the peak is heavy, the hub is open. Custom step: cut the X crossing to a flat joint when outlining.

Monochrome and background tests

In one color the node and pillars share a fill, and the silhouette still reads as an H with a peak. The accent is never required.

Why violet: blue is the default of software. A blue-violet is still clearly digital, reads as ambition rather than finance or utility, and gives the single node a distinct identity. Ratio: 85% neutral, 12% surfaces and structure, 3% accent. Gradients (Signal to a 20% lighter tint) are an optional expression for marketing only, never the logo. Contrast: #5A4BFF on white is about 6:1 and #8378FF on #0A0A0F about 6.5:1, both passing AA for text. Never put the full-color mark on mid-tone backgrounds below 3:1; switch to monochrome.

Typography

Sora

Brand and headings, weights 300, 600, 700. Geometric, wide, strong at display size. Weakness: loose in small text. Free (OFL). Alternative: Manrope.

Inter

Interface and body, weights 400, 500, 600. Excellent screen rendering and hinting. Neutral, so it lets Sora carry personality. Free (OFL). Alternative: Geist.

JetBrains Mono

Code, metadata, version labels, weight 400. Distinct letterforms for developer contexts. Free (OFL). Alternative: IBM Plex Mono.

Small-scale and platform use

Favicon

Micro mark, no wordmark. 16px: pillars and chevron only. 32 and 48px: same shape, SVG favicon with a prefers-color-scheme rule to flip ink between #0A0A0F and #fff.

App icon

Square on Apex Black, mark at 56% of the icon width, node in Signal Violet, OS applies corner radius (about 22%). Light variant: white tile, black mark.

Social

Mark centered on Apex Black at 52% of the frame, so circular crops keep the pillar edges.

Watermark

Monochrome mark with wordmark at 35 to 45% opacity, white on dark imagery and black on light, bottom-right.

Motion and loading

Logo reveal (700 ms)

Pillars rise from the baseline (0 to 280 ms), the chevron draws from left to right (200 to 500 ms), the node settles down onto the apex (450 to 700 ms). Easing: cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1). Direction is always upward and inward: the elevation and convergence ideas.

Loading loop: 1.4 s, CSS only. Replace with Framer Motion or Lottie using the same three steps. Respects reduced motion.

Interaction: 120 to 180 ms transitions, translate and opacity only. No glow, particles or 3D.

Visual language and UI

Everything derives from the mark: pillars become vertical rules and sidebar edges, the chevron becomes a 38° angled cut used on hero shapes, and the node becomes the one accent dot (status, focus, active tab). Radius: 8px controls, 12px cards, 4px badges, never pill-shaped containers. Spacing on an 4px base, 8px rhythm.

Buttons

Primary is inverted black or white. Accent is reserved for focus, links and the active node.

Empty and error states

Empty: "No projects yet. Create your first one." Error: "Deploy failed: build timed out after 10 minutes. Check the build log and retry." Plain, specific, no apologies.

Voice and taglines

Primary: Built with intention. Short, ownable, and it pairs directly with "Built by APEXHUB". Backups: Engineered ideas. / Where ideas get built. / Craft, shipped.

Rejected: "Build at the peak" (overpromises and ties to the mountain cliché), "Ideas. Code. Impact." (generic), "Building what's next." (futurist filler). Voice: a competent builder who explains clearly, avoids buzzwords, and lets the work make the claim.

Brand architecture

A master brand with descriptor endorsement: APEXHUB always leads, products follow as plain descriptors (APEXHUB Labs, APEXHUB Studio, APEXHUB Cloud, APEXHUB AI). Sub-products reuse the same mark, never new symbols, differing only by a small mono-set descriptor beside the wordmark. This prevents fragmentation and makes every launch build the same equity.

Guidelines

Sizes

Full logo: 120px wide recommended, 80px minimum. Horizontal: 100px, 64px minimum. Icon: 32px, 20px minimum. Micro mark: 16px recommended and minimum. Footer signature: 13px text, 11px minimum.

Correct use

Black on light, white on dark, node in Signal Violet only on neutral backgrounds with 3:1 contrast. One-color versions on photos. Clear space of 1x always.

Never

Stretch, squash, rotate or skew the mark.
Add glow, shadow, bevel or unapproved gradients.
Recolor pillars in the accent, or recolor the node randomly.
Retype or respace the wordmark, or rebuild the mark by hand.
Place the full-color mark on low-contrast or busy backgrounds.

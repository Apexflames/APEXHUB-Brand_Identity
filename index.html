Where the peak and the hub meet.

APEXHUB second-stage identity review. The original Ascent Node mark is kept, and refined where the review found weaknesses.

Review of Ascent Node

You chose to keep Ascent Node, so it stays. The review still found real weaknesses, and the refinements below target them instead of hiding them.

Weakness: genericness

A node, a chevron, and an A inside an H are all items the brief lists as generic. Fix: make the placement the signature. The node sits level with the pillar tops, with a fixed gap above the chevron apex.

Weakness: 16px

The 6.5-unit node falls to about 1px and disappears. Fix: a dedicated micro mark with a larger node that merges into the apex.

Weakness: A is forced

The A is only suggested by the chevron. Fix: stop claiming it. The H is primary, the rise is secondary, the A is a discovery.

The mark: Ascent Node

Primary meaning: an H of two pillars, with a node marking a single high point.

Secondary: the chevron is both the crossbar of the H and a peak rising between the pillars (APEX), and the pillars are the structure that connects (HUB). The chevron implies an A without drawing one.

Tertiary: a node and pillars give a structural, network feel without circuit imagery.

One sentence: an H whose center rises to a single point.

100-unit grid. X = pillar width = 16. Chevron stroke 14, node radius 6.5. Node center level with the pillar tops (y 15 against 14), 3.6-unit gap above the chevron apex. Pillars 72 tall, chevron apex at 36.

Optical notes: the chevron overlaps the pillars so the joins stay solid; the node is placed 1 unit lower than the pillar tops so it reads as aligned rather than floating above them. These are judgments from construction, to be checked in a vector editor at small sizes.

Logo system

Horizontal, stacked, symbol

Horizontal for navigation and wide layouts, stacked for square spaces and splash screens, symbol alone wherever the name is already visible.

Monogram

The mark itself is the A+H monogram, so no separate construction is needed. One symbol across every surface.

Micro mark

For 24px and below. Pillars widen from 16 to 22 units, the chevron flattens (apex at 50, ends at 80) and thins to 16, and the node grows to radius 11 and sits clearly apart above the apex. Rendered and compared at 16, 20, 24 and 32px in light and dark: the first version, with the node touching the apex, read as a W with a dot; this version reads closest to an H with a dot. Still a judgment from renders, not a test with users.

Wordmark

Outlined Sora Bold with optical pair spacing. Full details in the Wordmark section below.

Wordmark

The wordmark is Sora Bold converted to vector outlines, so spacing is fixed and cannot shift between browsers or fallback fonts. Every lockup on this page uses the outlined version.

Kerning

Base tracking is +60 on a 1000-unit em, which opens uppercase letters just enough for screens. Three pairs are then tightened optically: A-P (-18), E-X (-14), X-H (-10). The diagonals of A and X leave open space against straight stems, so those pairs sit closer. Stem pairs (P-E, H-U, U-B) keep the base value. These values are my optical judgment and should be reviewed in a vector editor at display and small sizes.

Mark and wordmark

In the horizontal lockup the wordmark cap height is about half the mark height, centered on the mark, with a gap of 2X (X = pillar width of the mark). The stacked lockup centers both and uses a gap of 2X as well. Minimum wordmark cap height: 8px.

Weight and customization

One weight, because APEX and HUB read as one name and a weight split gave no structural reason. The only customization so far is the spacing above. A custom X joint or terminal cut is possible later but is not designed yet. Lowercase is not used.

Monochrome and backgrounds

Without the accent, the node and pillars share one fill and the silhouette still reads as an H with a peak. The accent version colors only the node. On mid-tone backgrounds use whichever of white or black has more contrast (never the accent), and test light grey like #D9D9E0 with black. On photography use a one-color mark; on busy images place it on a solid containment tile.

Favicon, app icon, social, watermark

Favicon

Micro mark only, SVG with a prefers-color-scheme rule flipping the fill between #0A0A0F and #FFFFFF. 16px uses the micro geometry unchanged; 32 and 48px use the same file. Accent node only at 32px and above.

App icon

Black tile, mark at 60% width, node in accent. Light variant: white tile, black mark. The OS applies the corner radius; the preview uses 22%.

Social

Mark at about 52% of the frame on black; the circle crop keeps both pillars because nothing sits near the corners.

Watermark

Mark with wordmark, bottom-right, 14px from the edges, 8% of image width. White at 45% opacity on dark imagery, black at 40% on light.

Motion and loading

One principle: ascent

Pillars rise from the baseline, the chevron is drawn stroke by stroke from left to right, then the node floats up from below onto the apex. Total 700 ms, easing cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1). The order is the story: structure, connection, arrival. Exit: fade 150 ms.

Loading loop: 1.6 s, CSS only. Reduced motion: static mark, no loop. For long waits, add a text status beside it.

Visual language

Use the chevron angle (about 38 degrees from vertical) sparingly, for hero cuts and section dividers, as a principle rather than a rule. Pillars suggest vertical rules and sidebar edges, and the node becomes the single accent dot (active state, focus, status). Components stay conventional: 8px radius controls and 12px cards are suggestions, not requirements. The brand should guide a product, not dictate its shapes.

Dashboards

Neutral surfaces, accent only on active nav item, focus ring and primary data series. Mark top-left at 24px, footer signature at 70% opacity.

Developer tools and docs

System monospace for code and metadata. Mark in the header only; the signature lives in the footer. Content leads.

GitHub and portfolio

Org avatar uses the black app tile. README footer carries Built by APEXHUB as a single line. Portfolio hero leads with the work.

States

Errors and notices use text and icons with color, never color alone. Empty states say what to do next in one sentence.

Distinctiveness audit

Ascent Node is the more conventional of the two options. It combines three common ingredients (an H, a chevron, a node), so its ownability rests on the specific placement and proportions, not on the idea. Avoided on purpose: mountains, arrows, hexagons, circuits, sparkles, gradient blobs, and brackets. I cannot guarantee uniqueness: a text search cannot compare shapes. Run it through reverse image search and a trademark search before committing.

Name risk, unchanged

The name APEXHUB is in use by a UK digital marketing company, an ApexHub Labs repo, a racing-data app, and others (see the earlier search). A strong mark does not fix that. The mark does not depend on the name, so it can move to a new name if needed.

Voice, taglines, architecture

Voice: a capable builder explaining plainly. Short sentences, specific nouns, no superlatives. Words to use: built, engineered, shipped, intentional. Words to avoid: revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge.

Primary: Built with intention. Alternatives: Where the peak meets the hub. / Engineered to rise. / Build the next level. Others considered and dropped: Build at the peak, Ideas. Code. Impact., Technology with intention, Where ideas converge, Crafted for the digital world, Digital craftsmanship, Made to climb, One step higher, Built to connect, Ship higher. "Where the peak meets the hub" is the closest match to the mark's idea; "Built with intention" is the safer fit for the signature. Taglines are design judgments, not tested.

Architecture: branded house. APEXHUB leads; products are plain descriptors set in Inter beside the wordmark (APEXHUB Labs, Cloud, AI). They always use the same mark, a mono-colored descriptor, and no new accent colors. Products can differ by content, never by logo.

Guidelines

Clear space

X = pillar width. Keep 1X on all sides of the mark, and 1X between mark and wordmark. In the signature, 8px minimum around the lockup.

Minimum sizes

Full logo: 120 px wide recommended, 88 px minimum. Horizontal: 96 / 72. Stacked: 72 / 56 wide. Symbol: 32 / 20. Micro mark: 20 / 14. Signature: 13px text, 11px minimum. These are working recommendations to verify in real renders.

Never

Stretch, rotate, outline, or fill the mark with patterns.
Add glow, shadow, bevel, or gradients to the mark or node.
Move the node, change the chevron angle, or retype the wordmark.
Place the mark below 3:1 contrast, including the accent node on black.

Assets and not yet built

Deliver the structure from your brief with SVG as the source (the mark is three paths per variant, no effects), then transparent PNG, WebP, ICO and PNG favicons, and 192, 512, and 1024 app icons. Not built in this pass: the full mockup set (portfolio, mobile UI, GitHub, 404, presentation), exported files, a drawn wordmark outline, and real-device small-size checks. Say which to build next.
